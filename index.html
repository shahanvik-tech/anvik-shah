Coverage & Views

Where I've built a real base of knowledge, and what I currently think.

Consumer Durables

Cantor Fitzgerald · Mu-Rho Market Fund

The re-rating case for durables is usually underwritten on an existing-home-sales recovery. I think the replacement cycle rolling off the 2020 and 2021 demand pull-forward is the larger and more predictable driver, and it arrives regardless of where mortgage rates settle. The risk sits in mix, not volume: the group has defended price better than units, so the quarter to watch is the first one where units recover and average selling prices crack.

What would change my mind

A genuine move down in mortgage rates pulling housing-linked volume forward faster than replacement demand can account for. Right on direction, wrong on the driver.

Equity Market Structure

Goldman Sachs · One Delta & Prime Services

TXSE completed its phased rollout of all NMS symbols on July 31 with more than 50 member firms participating. Trading was always the tractable half of the problem: matching engine, connectivity, latency equalization. Listings are the real test, with ETPs late in Q3, corporate transfers in Q4, and IPOs targeted for 2027. Trading share can be bought through fee structure; a listing requires an issuer to accept a liquidity discount in exchange for something else, and that trade has to be argued rather than priced.

What would change my mind

A first cohort of corporate transfers larger than expected would reprice incumbent listing economics well ahead of my timeline.

Mid-Market M&A

Sellside Consulting Group

Deal flow here is gated by the bid-ask on quality, not by financing availability. Sponsors are holding assets past their intended horizon with marks that haven't been tested, owners of genuinely good businesses aren't compelled to sell, and everything else clears at a discount sellers won't accept. The unlock is structure, meaning earnouts, rollover equity and seller notes, rather than a move in rates. On the sell-side that puts the weight on diligence quality: the processes that close are the ones where quality of earnings survives the first pass.

What would change my mind

Material compression in credit spreads bringing strategics back in size would close the bid-ask on price alone and make the structure argument mostly irrelevant.

The Impact of COVID-19 on Emerging Economies

Mentored by Dr. Elaine Wah · Data Scientist, BlackRock AI Labs

The effect of COVID-19 on the Indian economy is well covered, but its effect on the "Make in India" scheme is not, despite that policy being one of the levers India could use to recover from the pandemic recession. I measured the effect by tracking how the economic variables behind the scheme moved after the onset of COVID-19, then worked through why they moved that way. The variables measuring the campaign's success trend positively over the period, alongside significant increases in India's PMI and foreign direct investment, and a decline in China's PMI against a rise in India's. On the evidence, the pandemic benefited the "Make in India" scheme.

  • MethodTime-series analysis of the scheme's underlying economic variables before and after the onset of the pandemic.
  • Key variablesIndia's Purchasing Managers' Index and foreign direct investment inflows, benchmarked against China's PMI.
  • FindingManufacturing activity and investment shifted toward India, indicating the pandemic accelerated rather than derailed the policy.
